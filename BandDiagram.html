Raising T excites electrons from the valence band across the gap, leaving holes behind (pair generation); lowering T lets pairs recombine (tick "Recombination" to also see it continuously at a fixed T). In n-type and p-type material the extra majority carriers are excited from the dopant level near EF (green dashed path) up to Ec (electrons) or down to Ev (holes). Particle counts are drawn on a log scale (109 to 1018 cm−3 maps to 0 to 80 particles) so the growth is visible, and in doped material at least 2 minority carriers are always shown, although the real minority density is many orders of magnitude lower; the exact n, p and ni are in the readouts. Model: Boltzmann statistics, dopants 0.05 eV from the band edge (they freeze out at low T; EF itself uses the full-ionization approximation), Nc = Nv = 1019(T/300)1.5 cm−3.
